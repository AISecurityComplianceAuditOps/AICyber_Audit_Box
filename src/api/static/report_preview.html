Definition of Risk Classifications:

The risk of an audit finding is determined by assessing the potential negative impact and the probability that it materializes. Audit findings are classified into four risk classifications.

High Risks

Non-adherence to XYZ Organization and Government Guidelines, Policies Approved by Competent Authority, ICT is not as per standard, high threat probabilities.

Medium Risks

These risks are important and management should quickly develop action plans that will ensure timely and permanent resolution of the weaknesses noted.

Low Risk

These risks are not material in the context of current levels of activity but management should be aware of them.

OK/ ACCEPTED

This is normal and good practice. It is as per the guidelines / best practices.

The question was never reaching the report. It lives in a requirement_question column that the export payload didn't send. Question-based rows also store the control id, the control name and the question as one combined string — “5.17 Authentication Information – Is password retry is configured as 3?” — so grouping on it raw gave every question its own group, headed by the whole question. The control prefix now heads the group and the question fills the cell.
